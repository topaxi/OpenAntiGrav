# Wipeout 2048's sky: an authored dome, not a cubemap

**A race now draws a sky.** Before this page, `just play 2048 --race` drew a
black one - `oag_raceplay::load`'s PS3/2048 branch called Wipeout HD's own
`hd_sky_model`, which looks for a `sky.gtf` sibling of the track, and 2048
ships no such file. Measured against `data/extracted/vita/PCSF00007` (the
decrypted EU package, base plus both DLC) on 2026-09-02.

## Summary

| Claim | Confidence |
| --- | --- |
| The sky is `skycube.rcsmodel`, a small authored dome, not a generated cube | 92 |
| It sits directly beside `track.vex`, not inside the `SkyCube/` subdirectory next to it | 95 |
| It decodes through the same `oag_rcs::rcsmodel::psp2` reader the track and craft already use | 92 |
| Its submesh record needed a second, previously-unrecognised buffer-pointer gap | 92 |
| The dome's authored radius is ~1.83 units, camera-centred, and needs rescaling to clear the near plane | 88 |
| Its diffuse texture is a single 4096x2048 `UBC2` panorama, one per circuit | 90 |
| `Lighting.Sky rotation` is degrees (Anulpha Pass: 230 in HD, -130 in 2048) | 85 |
| DLC circuits' skies and Zone's own sky swap are located and not wired | - |

## The shipped layout, read off the archive

Every base-game circuit (`altima`, `arena`, `bridge`, `cathedral`, `mall`,
`park`, `sol`, `subway`, `tower`) carries, beside `track.vex`:

```text
data/art/published/environments/<env>/skycube.rcsmodel        # the dome mesh
data/art/published/environments/<env>/skyParaboloid.gxt       # unread, see Open
data/art/published/environments/<env>/SkyCube/SkyCube.rcsmaterial
data/art/published/environments/<env>/SkyCube/SKYDOME_<Name>.gxt
```

**`skycube.rcsmodel` is not inside `SkyCube/`.** Only the material and its
texture are - a naming split this reading takes from the archive listing
itself rather than from the subdirectory name, which would have guessed
wrong. `oag_raceplay::load::environment::psp2_sky_sibling_name` rewrites the
track's own directory plus `skycube.rcsmodel`, the same shape
`oag_mesh::mesh::rcs::sibling_name` uses for the track's own `.rcsmodel`
sibling, but with a fixed basename rather than a stem rewrite - `track.vex`
and `track_reversed.vex` (where a circuit ships both) resolve to the same
sky, exactly as HD's `sky.gtf` does per circuit *directory*.

DLC1's four re-shipped HD circuits (`Anulpha_Pass`, `Chenghou_Project`,
`Moa_Therma`, `Vineta_K`) and DLC1's own four new ones (`Metropia`,
`Sebenco_Climb`, `Sol_2`, `Ubermall`) carry a differently-shaped
`skycube.rcsmodel` - six submeshes rather than one, textured from six
separate `SkyCube_<Name>_1..6.gxt` files rather than one panorama. Not wired
by this reading - see [Open](#open).

## The container is 2048's ordinary `.rcsmodel`, once a second record shape is found

`skycube.rcsmodel` opens `ed ad 5c ca`, the same magic
[2048-rcsmodel.md](2048-rcsmodel.md) already reads for `track.rcsmodel` and
every craft's `Ship.rcsmodel` - the same linker-style image, the same
relocation-table mechanism, the same material table found by scanning for
`.gxt`/`.rcsmaterial` text. On `altima`, `oag_rcs::rcsmodel::psp2::parse`
finds exactly one material (`skycube/skycube.rcsmaterial`, naming
`skycube/skydome_altima.gxt`) before any code change - the format-level
reading already generalises this far for free.

**What it did not find, until the record-pairing search grew a second known
gap: any geometry at all.** `submeshes()` locates a record by pairing two
GPU-pointer relocations exactly `BUFFER_POINTER_GAP` (28) bytes apart in the
CPU section, a shape measured across the whole 244,889-submesh track/craft
corpus. Every sky file's own pair sits **184** bytes apart instead - a
different record shape, not a wider version of the same one, evidenced across
every shipped sky file (18 checked directly: 9 base circuits, single-submesh;
9 DLC/dev-style, six-submesh, the first at the 184 gap and the remaining five
at the ordinary 28 - `crates/game/examples/vita_skycube_probe.rs` is the
reproducer) and closing, corpus-wide, **every** GPU pointer the 993-file sweep
previously left unpaired - see
[2048-rcsmodel.md#a-second-record-shape-closes-every-previously-unpaired-pointer](2048-rcsmodel.md#a-second-record-shape-closes-every-previously-unpaired-pointer)
for the full evidence and the corpus numbers. `oag_rcs::rcsmodel::psp2::SKY_BUFFER_POINTER_GAP`
is the constant; `submeshes()` tries both gaps per candidate pair rather than
replacing one with the other.

Once that second gap is tried, `altima`'s dome decodes cleanly: 480 triangles
over 287 vertices, stride 20, its declaration (found the same way
[2048-rcsmodel.md](2048-rcsmodel.md#section-b-carries-a-vertex-declaration-structurally-identical-to-hds)
already reads every other stride-20 file) naming `position` at `0x00`,
`normal` at `0x0c` and **`Uv1`** at `0x10` - a genuine diffuse texture
coordinate, not the `VertexColour1` that stride happens to carry on other
files in the corpus. Checked directly (`vertex_decl::find_by_stride` against
the dome's own CPU section, `crates/game/examples/vita_skycube_decl_probe.rs`)
before trusting `psp2::build`'s existing, declaration-driven texcoord path on
it: a wrong guess here would have painted
the dome from a colour channel read as UV, a plausible-looking picture on a
2:1 panorama texture and exactly the invented-not-measured failure
`CLAUDE.md` warns against.

## The dome is small, camera-centred, and needs rescaling

Every vertex on `altima`'s dome sits within 0.0003 units of radius 1.834 from
the model's own origin - a near-uniform sphere, not a hemisphere (`y` ranges
symmetrically ±1.834) - and the same shape recurs on every other base circuit
sampled. That is **not** Wipeout HD's convention: its generated cube
(`oag_mesh::mesh::sky_cube::HALF_EXTENT`) is 32 units, and Pulse's authored
skies ship at 18.4 to 61.7. `race::scene::frame` translates every sky model to
the camera each frame and applies no scale, so drawn at its authored size the
dome would clip: a visible point at angle `theta` off the view axis sits at
view-space depth `radius * cos(theta)`, and at `radius = 1.834` against the
near plane at `1.0`, anything past `arccos(1.0 / 1.834) ≈ 57°` off-axis clips
- a merely wide field of view reaches that at the screen corners.

`oag_raceplay::load::environment::psp2_sky_model` rescales the whole model
about its own centre to a fixed 32-unit radius after `psp2::build` returns it
- the same convention HD's cube uses, picked for consistency rather than
because the exact value matters: camera-centred size is appearance-invariant,
the same argument `sky_cube::build`'s own doc comment makes. It also zeroes
`lit` on every vertex - `psp2::build` defaults it to `1.0` for ordinary
geometry, but a sky is a picture of light, not a surface, the same rule
`sky_cube::build` states for HD's cube and Pulse's `_nolight` skies confirm
independently; left at `1.0` the stand-in light rig would shade one side of
the dome as if it caught a sun.

## The texture

`SkyCube/SKYDOME_Altima.gxt`: 4096x2048, format `0x86` (`UBC2`/`DXT23`,
already decoded per [gxt.md](gxt.md)), 8,388,672 bytes - the same size on
every base circuit sampled, decoding in 34ms. A 2:1 aspect is consistent with
an equirectangular panorama wrapped over the dome by its own authored `Uv1`,
though nothing here decodes the projection explicitly; the texture is bound
by material index exactly the way [2048-rcsmodel.md](2048-rcsmodel.md#which-submesh-uses-which-material-a-plain-index-0x18-bytes-before-the-record)
already reads for the track and craft, and needed no new binding logic.

## What it looks like

`just play 2048 --race` reports:

```text
Data\art\published\environments\altima\skycube.rcsmodel: the circuit's sky
dome, 480 triangle(s) over 1 submesh(es), 1/1 draw(s) textured from 1 of 1
material(s) (e.g. data/art/published/environments/altima/skycube/skydome_altima.gxt),
0 unresolved, 287 authored normal(s) (rest off face normals); 0 unaccounted
GPU pointer(s), rescaled from its authored radius of 1.83 unit(s) to 32 to
clear the near plane at any field of view
```

## Open

- **DLC's six-submesh skies are located, not wired.** `psp2_sky_model` reads
  whichever `skycube.rcsmodel` sits beside the chosen track, so a DLC circuit
  will draw *something* through the same path - but its six submeshes and six
  separate face textures are an unread mechanism (a real cubemap under a
  different name, by the shape of it, unlike the base game's single
  panorama), not confirmed to compose correctly through this reading.
- **`data/Tex/zoneSky.gxt` is not swapped in for Zone.** HD's own
  `Data/Tex/ZoneSky.gtf` swap
  ([zone-sky.md](../ghidra/functions/ps3-hdfury-eu/zone-sky.md)) has no
  counterpart wired here; a Zone race on 2048 draws the circuit's own dome.
- **`skyParaboloid.gxt`** (699,072 bytes, identical size on every circuit
  sampled) is unread. A dual-paraboloid reflection map is the obvious guess
  from the name and the fixed size across otherwise-different skies, and is
  exactly that, a guess.
- **The 156 bytes between the two record shapes' tails** (`BUFFER_POINTER_GAP`
  vs `SKY_BUFFER_POINTER_GAP`) are unread - see
  [2048-rcsmodel.md](2048-rcsmodel.md#a-second-record-shape-closes-every-previously-unpaired-pointer).
- **The panorama's projection** (equirectangular is inferred from the 2:1
  aspect and the authored `Uv1`, not confirmed against the executable).
- **`Sky brightness` and `Sky height offset`** are authored on every circuit
  (brightness 0.8 to 3.0, height offset -400 to 2000) and read by nothing
  here: their consumer in the executable is unlocated. The panorama is drawn
  at its own brightness and the dome stays centred on the camera.

## Sky rotation (2026-10-06, `2048-sky-fog`)

`oag_raceplay::load::environment::psp2_sky_model` now reads the circuit's
`Lighting.Sky rotation` out of `track.EnvSettings` and turns the dome by it
about the vertical, the same matrix `sky_cube::build` uses for HD's cube.
**Unit: degrees** - evidence: Anulpha Pass ships in both titles and authors
230 in HD and -130 in 2048, the same angle mod 360; the 2048 corpus spans -130
to 300. **Sign: HD's choice (positive about +Y), chosen, not measured** - no
reference frame exists for 2048 and the HD sign is itself unsettled (see
`sky_cube::build`). Pinned by
[psp2_sky_fog_ground_truth.rs](../../crates/game/tests/psp2_sky_fog_ground_truth.rs)
on all ten base circuits: every dome vertex keeps its height and horizontal
radius and moves round by the authored angle.

Judged as a player: the dome is rarely in view from the grid (altima, square
and bridge are enclosed at the start), so the turn is invisible in a start
frame; the screenshots are in the lane report.
