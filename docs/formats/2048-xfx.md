# 2048's `.xfx`: the same crossfade table, little-endian, five channels, indexed layers

**Container: 95 (all 23 files account to the byte). Bank per family: 90 (measured: every layer binds a looping waveform in that bank and in no other). Name hash: 95 (the loader's own function plus 32 names landing on 32 records). Channel law: channel 0 and the three zeros 82 (disassembly), channel 4's source unfound (chosen, no score). Kind-2 layers: 72 (decompile of the layer update, not run).**

Wipeout 2048 ships `data/audio/sound/xfship_<team>.xfx` in the base `data.psarc`:
**23 files**, magic `XFDX`, the same record layout as [HD's](hd-xfx.md) and read by
the same `oag_formats::xfx::Xfx`. The every-file test is
[`xfx_2048_ground_truth.rs`](../../crates/formats/tests/xfx_2048_ground_truth.rs);
the wired engine is
[`sfx_xfade_2048_ground_truth.rs`](../../crates/game/tests/sfx_xfade_2048_ground_truth.rs).

## What differs from HD (all measured, `xfx_2048_ground_truth.rs`)

| | HD (PS3) | 2048 (Vita) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| byte order | big-endian | **little-endian**, every field |
| `0x04` | `0x02060000` (platform 2) | `0x00060000` read little-endian (platform 0); the Vita loader (`FUN_8125e5d0`, v1.04) tests only the low 24 bits |
| channels | 4 | **5** (the Vita system is initialised for 5: `FUN_8125eea4(sys, 8, 0x20, 5, 0x30)`) |
| layer name | always set (`~jet01_02`) | **empty on the five `<team>2048` tables and Zone's**; the layer's `+0x18` `u16` is a cue index |
| layer kind (`+0`) | 0 | 0, **2 (a modulator)** on every ordinary `<team>2048` table, **1 (a stream)** once |
| smoothing | authored rates | **no smoothing on the ordinary tables** (every rate zero, every edge 511); Zone's tables smooth |
| triggers | 26 on channel 0 | 26 on channel 0 of HD-era and Zone tables, **none** on the five ordinary ones; none names a cue |

The reader is unchanged in structure: `Xfx` carries its byte order,
`parse` accepts exactly two version words (HD's and the Vita's), and every
accessor reads in that order. Every one of the 23 files accounts for every byte
and its coverage tiles with no gap.

## The 23 files

- **Five ordinary teams**: `xfShip_{ag_systems,auricom,feisar,piranha,qirex}2048.xfx`,
  four layers each (three looping cues and one kind-2 modulator), 8,908 bytes.
- **Five Zone tables**: `xfship_ZONE_{ag_systems,auricom,feisar,piranha,qirex}2048.xfx`,
  three or four layers, all on channel 0. Zone's Auricom table has one kind-1
  (stream) layer, cue 17, which the original starts through a stream callback
  this port has no counterpart for.
- **Thirteen HD-era tables** (`xfShip_assegai.xfx`, `xfShip_det.xfx`, ...): HD's
  layout re-encoded for the Vita, layers named, 5 or 4 layers each. `xfShip_feisar`
  is the exception inside the exception: its first three layers are indexed
  (cues 20, 22, 17) and only its afterburner is named. It is also the table the
  executable falls back to when a team's own file is missing
  (`FUN_81263f6c`: `xfship_feisar.xfx`).

The file names in the archive are spelled `xfShip_` and `xfship_ZONE_`;
`oag_assets` folds case, so the loader's `xfship_%s.xfx` and
`xfship_ZONE_%s.xfx` formats (strings at `0x814c515c` and `0x814c5118`) reach them.

## Which bank the layers address (90)

Measured, not read: for every `<team>2048` layer, the cue index resolves to a
**looping** waveform in `Ship_NGP.bnk` (99,360 to 270,608 bytes of PS-ADPCM at
31,998 Hz, one or two commands) and to a one-shot or a different sound in
`Ship_NGP_Zone.bnk` and `shipHD.bnk`. Zone's tables resolve to looping waveforms
in `Ship_NGP_Zone.bnk` and to one-shots in `Ship_NGP.bnk`. One Zone waveform
(Piranha, layer 3) is 16-bit PCM, which the SBlk reader decodes.

The executable's bank loader, `FUN_8121bce2`, names `Ship_NGP.bnk`
(`0x814a92a4`), `Ship_NGP_Zone.bnk` (`0x814a9390`) and `ShipHD.bnk`
(`0x814a93dc`) and picks by a selection field and the mode id; which circuit
takes which branch is unread (the "pack id" reading in
[zone-audio.md](../ghidra/functions/vita-2048-eu-v104/zone-audio.md) is a guess
about that field). **`oag_title::Crossfade` is the axis**: `ship` is
`Ship_NGP.bnk`, `ship_zone` is `Ship_NGP_Zone.bnk`, Zone's table infix is
`ZONE_`. The title's own `SoundBanks.ship` stays `shipHD.bnk` for every other
cue; moving those is [open](#open).

HD-era named layers resolve (by hash) in `Ship_NGP_Zone.bnk` and not in
`Ship_NGP.bnk`; so an HD hull on an ordinary 2048 grid (a guest-roster hull) has
a table and no sound: the loader reports `names no cue in Ship_NGP` per layer
and that craft is silent. Reading them against `shipHD.bnk` or the Zone bank
was tried and gives two or three of five layers, the jets unresolved, which is
a wrong engine rather than a quiet one.

## SBlk version 5 banks: names are hashes (95)

Every 2048 `.bnk` the engine reads is an SBlk block of **version 5**, where the
PS3's and PSP's is 3. The container (two sections) is the same; the name table is
not. `Bank::sound_names` walks 16-byte names and finds none; the Vita table is
`cue_count` records of 16 bytes from `+0x14` of the name block:

```text
+0x00  u32  hash of the name
+0x04  u16  cue index
+0x06  u16  next record in the collision chain, 0xffff at the end
+0x08  u32, u32  not read
```

followed by the pool of the names themselves. The hash is **FNV-1 seeded with
zero**: `h = h * 0x01000193 ^ byte`, from `h = 0` (`FUN_81352b9c`; confidence 95,
because 32 names from the HD-era tables and the weapons bank land on records
whose cue is the one the tables expect). `Bank::cue_named` uses it where
`Bank::is_hashed` (descriptor version 5), `Bank::cue_by_hash` is the lookup.
`Ship_NGP.bnk` carries 16 bytes past its waveform section (`00 07 00 00`, then
zeros); `sblk::TAIL_SLACK` lets one block of tail through.

Of the 29 banks under `data/audio/sound/`, 18 parse. The eight
`env_{altima,arena,bridge,cathedral,sol,square,subway,tower}.bnk` fail the
section framing; `speech_zone_NGP.bnk`, `speech_fe_NGP.bnk` and
`Speech_NGP.bnk` fail the whole-ADPCM-block check, so their waveform section is
not PS-ADPCM as a whole ([open](#open)). `generaltrack.bnk` and
`speech_PreRaceChatter.bnk` are version 3 and keep their 16-byte names.

## The layer kinds (72)

`FUN_8125d4b6` starts a layer by its kind byte: **0** plays a cue, by name when
the name's first byte is set and by cue index (`+0x18`) when it is not; **1**
starts a stream through a registered callback ("Attempt to start a stream, but
not callback is registered" is its error string). `FUN_8125d61e`, the layer
update, handles **2** by writing the layer's gain and pitch into the slots of the
element `+0x12` names: gain into `+8` (a multiplier) and pitch into `+0x34` (an
offset). A modulator plays nothing. On every ordinary `<team>2048` table layer 3
is a kind-2 layer on channel 4 linked to layer 0 (the idle loop): a gain of
`646/1024` and a pitch that climbs from `417` to `628` over channel 4's first
fifty counts. `xfade.rs` applies it as a gain multiplier and a bend offset on
layer 0, **one tick late as the original's evaluation order is** (layer 0 runs
before layer 3).

**Chosen, not measured**: the modulator's gain reaches the target as the curve
value alone; the original's is itself a product with the modulator's own two
slot floats, whose values were not read.

## The per-tick law (82 for the read terms)

Two update functions write the channels. `FUN_811cb9ac` is the base ship class's
(vtable `0x81511d00`): channels 0 to 3, HD's law
([xfade.md](../ghidra/functions/ps3-hdfury-eu/xfade.md)): the HD-era tables'
class. `FUN_812cd154` is the update the **five team classes** share (the same
function pointer in five vtables, `0x815166e4`, `0x815167f8`, `0x815168d8`,
`0x815169f0`, `0x81516ad0`; the strings `Feisar2048`, `Qirex2048`,
`AG_Systems2048`, `Auricom2048`, `Piranha2048` sit in it):

```text
speed_field = 3.6 * *(*(ship + 0x5f84) + 0x428)           ; the same quantity as HD's
channel 0   = clamp(trunc(0.5 * speed_field * class), 0, 510)
channels 1, 2, 3 = 0
channel 4   = clamp(trunc(70 * ship[+0x6098]), 0, 510)
```

with `class` `1.25`, `1.15`, `0.95`, `0.8` for the race's class word
(`DAT_8153fd18` 1 to 4) and untouched otherwise, and every channel clamped at
`0x43ff0000`, 510.0, where HD clamps at 511. No `X` term and no hover probe.

Not recovered: what `ship[+0x6098]` is (the executable also derives the gear
state and `xpress`/`xrelease` from it, and `100.0` means pedal held there).
**This port feeds channel 4 `70 * throttle / 100 / 0.75` for the player and
writes nothing for an opponent, and holds `class` at 1.0: chosen, not measured.**
`0.75` is `DAT_8151fdd0` (0.7 in the Detonator mode), the scale the executable
divides by in the same function.

Consequence, and it is the lane's honest ceiling: channels 1 to 3 are zero, so
layer 1 (gain 0 at channel value 0) never sounds on an ordinary table, layer 2
needs channel 4 above about 250 (a 70-scale pedal cannot reach it), and layer 0
fades to nothing by channel 0 of about 150. **What the crossfade alone gives a
2048 craft is the low-speed note on the grid and in the first seconds, with the
pedal bending its pitch.** Cruising is the gearbox below.

## The gearbox: the other half of the engine, not wired

The same function plays, by name, `~Ship<N>_<Low|Mid|High|vHigh><Acc|Dec>_<1..3>`
and the same with `_G_`, plus `Ship_Gear`, `Ship_Gear_up`, `INT_gearchange`,
`Land_antigrav`, `flaps_l`/`flaps_r`, `xpress`, `xrelease` and
`c_AfterburnerIg`, out of `Ship_NGP.bnk` (`DAT_818c4de0`). `N` is 1 for
`Feisar2048`, 2 `Qirex2048`, 3 `Piranha2048`, 4 `Auricom2048`, 5
`AG_Systems2048`. **251 of the 254 names built from that grammar resolve in
`Ship_NGP.bnk`** (the hash lookup above), so the cues exist. The state machine
(five gears, a random variant 1 to 3, two voices cross-faded by the pedal's
change, an up-shift when the sample ends) is decompiled but not ported; it is
the first open item below.

## Checked against the other titles

- **Omega**: no `.xfx` in any of its nine archives; its ten old-container `.bnk`
  are SBlk version 3 (`is_hashed` false), so `cue_named` takes the old path
  there. Engine audio is Wwise. *Checked, differs.*
- **HD**: the reader is unchanged for HD's big-endian files (`xfx_ground_truth.rs`
  and `sfx_xfade_ground_truth.rs` pass). *Ported, HD unaffected.*
- **2048's own SFX**: `cue_named` working on version-5 banks turns on every cue
  the title's `SoundBanks` already named: a 2048 race's load report went from 42
  cues not loading to 6. They are read out of `weapons.bnk`, `speech.bnk`
  and `shipHD.bnk` (HD's names for the same sounds, "chosen" by an earlier
  lane); `Weapons_NGP.bnk` and `Speech_NGP.bnk` are the 2048-named banks.
