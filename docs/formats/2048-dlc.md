# Wipeout 2048's downloadable campaigns on our menus

Read 2026-10-26, lane `2048-dlc-menus`. Wiring, not recovery: the executable
side is [frontend-campaign-map.md](../ghidra/functions/vita-2048-eu-v104/frontend-campaign-map.md)
(the hotspots, the projection, the mount gate), the packages' contents are
[patches.md](patches.md), and the cells are Wipeout HD's own, read by
[campaign-screens.md](../ui/campaign-screens.md)'s loader. What this page adds
is where the original offers the circuits, what a mounted pack changes in this
build, and what was chosen rather than measured.

## Where the original offers the DLC circuits

| Surface | The original | Confidence |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| The campaign map | Two more campaigns, **HD** (`grid0`..`grid7`) and **Fury** (`grid8`..`grid15`), each a hotspot per `PI_Cell` on the map the single-player events sit on, opened by the `FE_RC_HD` and `FE_RC_FURY` tiles `data2.psarc`'s `NEWGUI/Definition.xml` adds to `GameModeChoice`. The hotspots exist only while the matching pack is mounted: `g_bDlc1Mounted` and `g_bDlc2Mounted`, written once, by `Boot_CheckDlcPackageFlags`, from `DLC1W2048PACKAGE` and `DLC2W2048PACKAGE` | 84 (immediates read, no live trace) |
| Single race or track select | **Not offered offline.** 2048 has no single-race screen. The one track grid the patched file authors (`CP_TrackGrid`, a `TouchTrackGrid`) sits on `Home`, beside the race-type, speed-class and track-select buttons of the online game-creation chain; which circuits it lists is filled at run time and is unread | 60 (read off the XML, not walked on a Vita) |
| A circuit's own pages | The photo is `Data\FE\NewImages\trackscreens\<circuit>` and the emblem the circuit's own `fe\TrackSelectEmblem`, both `.gxt` on the disc (the front end's `.gtf` name is the same texture); a reversed circuit shows its forward twin's photo. Both exist for all twenty circuits the cells race | 85 (listed in the packs and the patch, drawn on a card for Vineta K and Talon's Junction) |

So "reachable from the menus where the original offers them" is the campaign map
and nothing else. This build's RACE BOX has a track picker of its own (the one
every title has, not a 2048 screen), and it lists whatever the catalogue holds.

## What a mounted pack changes here

`oag_title::AddOn` is one downloadable archive: its name, the circuit list it
carries, the grids it opens, the `Campaign=` tag those grids carry, the tile
that opens them, and the craft the campaign flies. `oag_2048::ADD_ONS` holds
`dlc1.psarc` (`Campaign="HD"`, `FE_RC_HD`) and `dlc2.psarc` (`"Fury"`,
`FE_RC_FURY`). Another title's `ArchiveCandidates::add_ons` is empty and nothing
reads it.

1. **The catalogue.** `oag_assets::Archives::mount_add_ons` reads each mounted
   pack's own `Data\art\published\DLC1\plugins\tracks\Definition.xml` into the
   circuit manifests. The base `data.psarc` ships a 16-circuit, forward-only
   skeleton of that file and the geometry of four circuits (`Vineta_K`,
   `Anulpha_Pass`, `Chenghou_Project`, `Moa_Therma`), so reading it by name, or
   keeping whatever has geometry, offers DLC circuits with no pack. The packs
   carry the identical full copy: 28 `PI_Track`s, eight forward and eight `_R`
   for the HD pack's circuits, four and four for Fury's, and `Zone_1`..`Zone_4`.
   With both packs the boot lists 38 circuits (the 10 native and 28), with
   neither 10. Every consumer of `catalogue::documents` (the RACE BOX, the remix
   catalogue) gets this with no change.
2. **The tiles.** `Frontend::set_tiers` is given one `Tier` per mounted pack and
   the tile of the same name opens it. A tile whose pack is not mounted keeps
   its note ("is another title's campaign") and stays on the grid: nothing
   changes without the pack.
3. **The map.** Built from the title's own grids: each `PI_Cell` is a node,
   placed by `AddOn::nodes`, an `oag_title::NodeMap` whose numbers are `oag_2048::campaign::tier::NODES` (scale 3 by 2, bias 55 and 25, the
   sixteen per-grid offsets: the executable's immediates, confidence 84). The
   group wordmarks the file authors (`UpliftLabel` ... `AftermathLabel`, sixteen
   `<CanvasLabel>`s in `data2`'s `NEWGUI/Definition.xml`) each sit with their
   bottom edge within one canvas unit of the first row of their grid's nodes and
   over its columns, for all sixteen grids: the file's layout agreeing with the
   projection (`vita_2048_dlc_menus_ground_truth`).
4. **A launch.** `Launch::Cell(name)` finds the cell in the mounted packs' grids
   and goes through the same `launch_campaign_cell` every other title's campaign
   cell does. Ship Select is skipped, because it lists the native roster and the
   campaign flies the HD roster's `Feisar` (`AddOn::default_team`), the craft of
   `hdships`; 2048's own `feisar2048` is not that ship.

## The census

| | HD pack | Fury pack |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Grids / cells | 8 / 87 | 8 / 80 |
| Race | 28 | 16 |
| Speed Lap | 22 | 12 |
| Time Trial | 22 | 0 |
| Tournament | 7 | 5 |
| Elimination | 0 | 18 |
| Zone | 8 | 4 |
| Detonator, Nitro Battle (refused) | 0 | 12, 13 |

Every one of the 142 cells whose mode this engine runs resolves to a `track.vex`
(or `track_reversed.vex` for an `_R` circuit) that a mounted archive holds; the
25 Detonator and Nitro Battle cells are refused for their mode and drawn closed.
HD's Zone cells race its ordinary circuits and Fury's race `Zone_1`..`Zone_4`.
Which pack a campaign needs is verified by a circuit census: every circuit the HD
grids race is in `dlc1.psarc` or the base package, every one the Fury grids race
is in `dlc2.psarc`.

## Chosen, not measured

- **The unlock law.** A grid is open when it authors no lock, when it has earned
  points, or when the grid its `<Unlock Grid>` names has earned its
  `RequiredPoints`: HD's own campaign law (`CampaignStage::grid_is_unlocked`),
  inherited. 2048's executable gate on these nodes is unread.
- **A cell this build cannot race is drawn closed**, so a player is not offered a
  card whose launch would refuse; `--unlock-all` opens grids and leaves these.
- **The icon a mode draws.** 2048 has four mode glyphs; Detonator and Nitro Battle
  borrow the nearest.
- **The pixel scale** of a canvas unit, and that the bias is left off (it moves a
  whole cluster, not one node against another).
- **The craft.** `Feisar` for both campaigns, the HD roster's first; the Fury
  roster's own default is unmeasured.
- A map opens on its first open node, not on a closed one the file lists first.
- **No difficulty rung.** HD's cell screen lets a player pick one; a launch from
  this map carries none, so a cell that authors targets per rung (Time Trial,
  Speed Lap, Zone) is judged at `Medium`, the rung `evaluate_medal` always fell
  back to, and the AI runs on the default skill curve. A medal then reaches the
  save under the campaign's own key and opens the grid it unlocks
  (`medals_earned_on_one_grid_open_the_next`).
- **Ship Select is skipped.** The cells author `ShipChoice="Yes"`, but the screen
  lists the native roster; choosing among HD's own ships is not offered.

## Left open

- **Circuit names.** A DLC circuit's card shows its id (`Vineta_K`). The packs
  author none, and the string table carries `NN_TRACK` entries (`01_TRACK` reads
  `VINETA K`); the pack's `FEModelLocation` ends `track01` for the eight HD
  circuits, but names `track01` again for `Zone_1`..`Zone_4` and a folder name
  for Fury's, so that link names four wrong circuits and is not taken. The rule
  the original uses is unread.
- **A cell's race played to its end** (the medal screen and the return to the
  map) is untried; the launch is walked (below), the medal law is HD's.
- **The group wordmarks** (`canvasTextureHD.gxt`) are not drawn.
- **`CP_TrackGrid`'s contents**, the one circuit list the original keeps for a
  chosen race.

## Walked

Windowed on a software-Vulkan Xvfb with no profile of the maintainer's, by
pointer alone: the `HD CAMPAIGN` tile, its first node (Vineta K, Race), `Launch`.
The race loads `Data\art\published\DLC1\environments\Vineta_K\track.vex` and
`Data\art\published\hdships\Feisar\Ship.vex`, eight craft, lap 1 of 3. Stills of
the maps and cards are `--until map:grid0_3_1`, `card:1:grid8_3_1` and the like on
the package: this title has no `--menu-page track-select`, because it authors no
track-select screen.

## Omega and HD (checked)

**Omega, checked, differs.** Omega ships all 38 circuits in its base package and
reaches HD's and Fury's campaigns through HD's own `Campaign Selection` list:
`<Entry item="campaignList" playGoChunk="1" equals="FE_RC_HD" goto="Grid Selection">`
and `playGoChunk="3"` for Fury ([omega-frontend.md](omega-frontend.md)). The gate
is a PlayGo install chunk, not a mounted pack, so `oag_omega::TITLE.archives.add_ons`
is empty and its campaign launch already resolves these cells
(`omega_campaign_launch_ground_truth`). Nothing here ports.

**HD, checked, applies, not wired.** The cells are HD's own and its campaign
screens already read them; the `AddOn` mechanism is for a title whose circuits
arrive in a separate archive, which HD's are not.
