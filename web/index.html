OpenAntiGrav

A clean-room reimplementation of the Studio Liverpool anti-gravity racing engine. It plays your own disc image: pick one below. The image is read here, in this browser tab, and is never uploaded anywhere.

A Wipeout Pulse or Wipeout Pure PSP image (.chd or .iso) is what this web build is meant for. The image is read a piece at a time, as the game asks for it.

Needs WebGPU (Chrome or Edge 113+, Safari 26+, Firefox 141+ on Windows). Keyboard and gamepad; no sound or movies yet. Source · Nightly downloads
