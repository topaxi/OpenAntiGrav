OpenAntiGrav

A clean-room reimplementation of the Studio Liverpool anti-gravity racing engine. It plays your own disc image: pick one below. The image is read here, in this browser tab, and is never uploaded anywhere.

The image is read a piece at a time, as the game asks for it. .chd and .iso are what a browser can open (an encrypted PS3 .iso also needs its key); the table below says which discs get how far.

You can also drop the image, and a PS3 disc's key file, anywhere on this page.

This disc needs its key

A PS3 disc image is encrypted. Its key is a small .dkey or .key file, or 32 hex digits. It is used in this tab only and is never uploaded.
