# Pure's soundtrack plays, and both titles' track names are recovered - Pulse's are deliberately unwired

2026-08-17. A Pure boot used to come up silent because `MusicDiscs::survey` resolved the booted source against `oag_pulse::TITLE`, whose deny-list names **both** Pure pressings, so it returned `WrongTitle`. Five call sites had the same hard-coded `oag_pulse::open`; they now go through `crate::title::open_source`. **Both halves came off the disc, neither by analogy with Pulse.** `strings` on Pure's `BOOT.BIN` puts `Data\Music\frontend.at3`, `%s\%s` and `music.at3` next to `c:/Work/Wipeout/Code/System/Sound/MusicManager.cpp`; Pure's front-end path is a **literal** where Pulse expands a `frontend%d.at3` template, and **none of Pulse's eight expansions hashes on either Pure pressing** - a different shape, not a different spelling. The soundtrack directories are declared in `Data\Plugins\PI001\Definition.xml` as `PI_Music` nodes beside the `PI_Track` and `PI_Team` ones - nineteen, all resolving on both pressings. **The bigger finding: Pulse declares its sixteen the same way and every one resolves too** (`Data\Music\Aphex\music.at3` -> `f38af1b6`). `ps2-audio.md` had "the names" as *not determined*; that is now half-answered - the missing piece was never a per-track name, it is `music.at3` on both discs and the *directory* varies. **Pulse is still found by what its entries are, on purpose** - `oag_title::Music::tracks` is `None` for it, because switching over reorders its race playlist and needs its own before/after evidence. `pulse_is_still_found_by_what_its_entries_are` fails the day someone flips it. **The trap this opened and closed in the same change**: with the deny-list no longer stopping a Pure disc dead, nothing stopped `pure-psp-eu.chd` being offered as `pure-psp-usa.chd`'s counterpart - matching track counts and lengths mean `Soundtrack::pairs_with` would have *passed*, and only a corpus accident (no Pure PS2 release) saved it. `find_release` now takes the booted `&Title` and refuses a different one; `a_pure_boot_finds_no_counterpart_release` is the tripwire. **Where the code went**: `crates/game/src/music.rs`, because `audio.rs` sits at a frozen `BASELINE` ceiling. Verified on real discs both ways: Pure plays `frontend.at3` at 40.1 s looping and a 326.1 s race track, Pulse still plays `frontend1.at3` at 29.6 s. `crates/game/tests/pure_music_ground_truth.rs` (7 tests), [pure-status.md](../docs/formats/pure-status.md#music-recovered-by-name-and-played). **Still open**: which track a menu or a circuit plays is unestablished on either title, so `MUSIC_TRACK` is a fixed 0; Pure's `MusicSelection_Screen` and `Music Order` are named in `.rodata` and unread; (Pure's banks *are* decoded and playing as of 2026-08-23; the "no `SBlk`" claim this row carried was wrong.)
