# Wipeout HD's front end boots, off a chain that says it is only declared

2026-08-17, and the shape of it is the part to read: **the blocker was a type, not evidence.** `oag_hd::TITLE.front_end` was `None` because `oag_title::BootProfile::chain` was defined as a *measurement* and HD's order is a *declaration* read out of its own `skin.xml`, so the only two states available were "refuse a front end whose layout is confidence 92" and "put a hypothesis in the one field contracted never to hold one". [ADR-0025](../docs/architecture/adr/0025-a-boot-chain-carries-its-provenance.md) is the third answer: `BootProfile` gains a required `Provenance` of `Measured` or `Declared`, both PSP titles are the former, HD is the latter, and `boot::load_shell` prints *"this order is what its front-end XML declares, not a boot anyone has watched"* near the top of every such boot. **Nothing about HD became more certain** - the order, whether the picker runs at all on a PS3 taking its language from the XMB, and which of the six `skin.xml` copies is live are all exactly as open as before, and each is still one emulator capture away. What is honestly lost is a guarantee: `None` made a wrong screenshot impossible by making every screenshot impossible, and what replaces it is a label plus `oag-hd`'s own test, which fails if anyone sets `Measured` without a capture behind it. **Three more axes moved into the title package on the way, each because HD disagreed rather than because it looked tidy**: `FrontEnd::root` (HD's front-end plugin is *named*, `Data\Plugins\Frontend\Gui\Skin.xml`, where both PSP titles use the numbered `PI001`), `FrontEnd::language_plugins` (sixteen named plugins against five numbered ones), and the provenance itself. Each was a constant in `oag-pulse` that every title reached for. **What actually drew** once it booted: HD's screens in their own 1920x1080 grid (`frontend::Space::HD` - `Space` was already the mechanism, built for the PS2's grid, so `MenuSkin` needed no change after all), 15 of 16 languages, 1,602 German strings, and `Studio Logo` playing the [Studio Liverpool reel](../docs/formats/bik.md). Six of the nine declared screens - a connection check, three dialogs, an EULA, a save warning - are stepped over and **named on the report**, which is ADR-0023's filter doing the job it was built for, on the first title to drop more steps than it keeps. **Two bugs this found, both pre-existing and neither about HD.** (1) `capture.rs` chose between the boot's two movies by asking *"is this the screen the picker confirms into?"*, which was **never the same question**: `language_confirm_target` is the step *after* the picker, which on Pure is `Developer Publisher Screen` - the screen that plays movie **zero** - so the old form selected the second movie for the first movie's screen on Pure too. It survived there because a capture of that screen is byte-for-byte identical either way (verified by MD5 across the fix, both cuts being 480x272 and the parent's fills covering the frame), and it did not survive on a chain with one movie whose logo screen *is* the after-language screen. It handed the capture `after_language_movie`, which on HD is `None`, so the renderer was built with no video pipeline and every screenshot came out black with the frame counter drawn over it. Now asked as "is this the chain's second movie screen?", which is the same question `boot::assemble` answers when it hands each step its plan. (2) `boot::movies::load_movie` was WAD-shaped throughout - `index_of_hash`, `entry_len`, `peek` - so a PSARC source could not read a movie at all; `load_movie_by_path` is the second route, branched on the container rather than the console. **Four findings were recorded and not chased here, and all four have since been chased** - see [hd-frontend.md](../docs/formats/hd-frontend.md). In the order they were written: HD's `.fnt` files *are* the byte-order story, so its front end no longer draws in 5x7; `boot::load_sprites` routes a `.gtf` through `oag_formats::gtf` and `saveIcons`, `line` and `Title_Arrow_HD` are on screen; the three plugins reporting `Svenska` are still the disc's own copy-paste and still left alone (there are four of them - Swedish is the fourth and is correct); and Portuguese parses, its `definition.xml` being Latin-1 in a mixed-encoding release rather than a schema fault, so the count is sixteen of sixteen. The counts below are this row's own and are left as measured on the day. `crates/game/tests/hd_boot_ground_truth.rs` (7 tests, all disc-backed), [hd-frontend.md](../docs/formats/hd-frontend.md). **The main menu named as next here is done** - see [hd-frontend.md](../docs/formats/hd-frontend.md#the-main-menu-is-horizontal-and-it-is-drawn-that-way-now) (and, for a 2026-09-01 capture of it running, [hd-frontend.md](../docs/formats/hd-frontend.md#the-fe-style-switch-is-live-works-and-is-not-an-archive-swap---confirmed-by-capture)) - so what remains is the capture that upgrades `Declared` to `Measured`.

## Open

- The actual boot order, whether the picker runs at all on a PS3 (which takes its language from the XMB), and which of the six `skin.xml` copies is live are all still unresolved - each is one emulator capture away.
- HD's boot chain provenance is `Declared`, not `Measured` - nothing about its real boot behaviour is more certain than before this change.
- **2026-08-30, partial: two of the open questions above are narrowed, not the type.** `scripts/rpcs3-drive.py`'s new `browse` subcommand (and `Session.wait_for_screen_pressing`, needed because `EpilepsyWarning`'s live path is a `<Dialog>` that nothing advances without a press) got a boot's `TTY.log` reading `"" -> Top -> Language Selection -> PreFMVConnect -> Studio Logo -> EpilepsyWarning -> FirstPlay -> Save Warning -> ...  -> Main Menu`. That answers "does the picker run" (yes) and "which of `EpilepsyWarning`'s two paths is live" (the dialog) - see [hd-frontend.md](../docs/formats/hd-frontend.md#the-boot-chain-declared-and-then-watched), confidence 80 there, capped because it is RPCS3 rather than the EBOOT or hardware and only the `DATA00`/`DATA05`/`DATA06` chain family was exercised. It does **not** touch which `skin.xml` copy is live, or `BootProfile`'s own `Provenance` - this was a `TTY.log` screen-name read, not the systematic per-screen capture `Measured` needs.
