# Wipeout HD / Fury's HUD reads, and the reader it shares with Pulse was wrong in three ways Pulse's own data could never have shown

2026-08-17. All **eighteen** of HD's shipped HUD layouts compose with **nothing missing and nothing skipped**, into 2,320 widgets - cross-checked against a second walker written in the test file, because Pulse's `grep`-the-file trick does not survive a layout that is 17 files. `oag_hd::hud`, `oag_game::hud::compose`, `crates/game/tests/hd_hud_ground_truth.rs`, [hd-hud.md](../docs/formats/hd-hud.md). **HD's HUD is Pulse's HUD composed out of fragments**: a mode's root is a shell of 1-2 KiB that pulls in up to sixteen more by `<LoadXML SrcRel=... DirectEmbed="true">`, 720 of 720 includes on the disc spelled that way and none with `Src`. **Three rules, each of which Pulse's own five layouts are silent about.** (1) **`SrcRel` resolves against the including file's directory**, which is the only thing making the three skins distinct - `/data/xml/elimination_hud.xml` and `/data/xml/wo3_hud/elimination_hud.xml` both name `HUD_elim_line.xml` and mean different files - and **`..` ships**: Duel reaches four fragments as `..\HUD_colours.xml`, so a reader that concatenates loses a quarter of that mode's HUD with an error that reads like a missing file (5 files -> 9 once the segment is applied). (2) **`OffsetX`/`OffsetY` compose down the tree and `x`/`y` do not.** The walker read an `<Item>`'s offset as *absolute* on the stated grounds that "nothing in the shipped data nests them" - true of Pulse (26 offsets, all `<Item>`, 0 nested) and false of HD (218 offsets, 116 of them on widgets, 242 widgets holding widgets). (3) **A nameless widget is drawn, not broken.** Both widget readers required a `name` and recorded the loss in `skipped`; Pulse names all of its, HD leaves **106** anonymous against 2,214 named, mostly `BackgroundLayer="1"` panels behind a named readout. **And the layouts name source art rather than shipped files**: `hdHUD.mip` (148 sprites) and `detonator_hud2.tga` (44) are the two most-used references and neither extension exists on the disc - both resolve with the extension replaced by `.gtf`, so 12 of 12 references land instead of 10. `oag_hd::hud::texture_entry`, confidence 85, **no executable path read for it**. **One thing to know before continuing.** `docs/formats/hd-hud.md`'s counts are the composition *this build* performs: six of the eighteen roots ship in **more than one archive at different sizes** (`elimination_hud.xml` is 1,856 bytes in `DATA00` and 5,666 in `DATA02`), `oag_assets::Archives` picks by this project's own precedence, and **whether the original picks the same copy is unread** - the test pins it per root so a change to the archive order shows up there rather than in a screenshot. Both of this row's holds - no `.gtf` decoder, no pixel drawn - are discharged.
