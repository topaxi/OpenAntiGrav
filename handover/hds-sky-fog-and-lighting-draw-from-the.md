# HD's sky, fog and lighting draw from the disc's own statements - three holds remain, each named where it lives

2026-08-18, on branch `worktree-hd-talons-fidelity`, judged against an rpcs3 frame of the same Talon's Junction grid. Three landings: the `sky.gtf` cubemap drawn on a camera-centred cube through the existing `Depth::Sky` path (`oag_render::mesh::sky_cube`; `Sky rotation` applied as degrees, the unit the corpus forces); race fog on the curve read out of the circuit materials' own fragment microcode - `exp(-(fogColour.w * view_depth)^2)`, `fogColour` a crc32 **preimage** - and the lit equation likewise: `ambient + prelitScale * lightmap^prelitPower + sun * NdotL * lightmap.a`, plus `NdotH^32` specular, the lightmap's alpha being a baked shadow mask. The tool that opened this is [`scripts/ps3-microcode.py`](../scripts/ps3-microcode.py); census and listings in [renderer.md](../docs/ghidra/functions/ps3-hdfury-eu/renderer.md#the-race-fog-curve-is-read-out-of-the-circuit-materials-own-microcode). **The three holds**: whether `fogColour.w` is `Fog.Fog Density` unscaled (passed through unscaled at 60; the PPU fill is unread, and the binder keys names through `Crc32_HashString` with no immediate hashes, so finding it means walking the name table at `0x008b7f40`); the sky rotation's **sign** (nothing on the disc checks it; a landmark in an rpcs3 capture would); and HD's **exposure stage**, which is the one term of the chain that is not the disc's: the authored branch shades in linear light per [ADR-0026](../docs/architecture/adr/0026-hd-authored-lighting-is-linear.md), with saturate-and-encode standing in for the unread adaptive exposure (`Tone adaption boost`/`Tone maximum brightness` read and not understood; a sweep of the EBOOT's 75 post fragment programs found no tone curve, so the exposure is likely a PPU-computed scale). Measured on the reference framing: clipped share 13.2 % -> 7.4 % against the reference's own 12.6 %, and the track's authored panel detail visible again. **A fourth gap has a full reading and no implementation**: the rainbow strips beside the road are `etched_glass_tech.rcsmaterial` - material row 417 names it, and material rows carry their own `.rcsmaterial` path, so per-material microcode classification is *possible at load* - whose fragment programs sample `dc_iridescent_gradient.gtf` at `dot(N, V)` (a dependent read, decoded instruction by instruction), while this renderer paints the LUT by mesh UV. **The whole post chain is read AND implemented (2026-08-19)**: the HD race renders into a linear `Rgba16Float` scene target (`oag_render::post::hd_bloom::Chain`; every scene shader switches to linear output off the target format via a `linear_out` pipeline constant - `mesh_render::is_linear_target`), and the chain runs the read passes verbatim: downsample ladder, GPU-side adaptation (`adapted += rate * (luma(mean) - adapted)`, first frame jump-started for single-frame captures), the gate with its adaptation fade (`contribution.w = 0` and `additiveColour = 0` are the *engine's own* fills, `FUN_003b4690` read end to end), quarter-res nine-tap blurs at `authored size / buffer size`, and the resolve with the **read exposure** `scale = Tone max brightness - min(Tone adaption boost * adapted, Tone darkening clamp)` (`FUN_003e3268`; registrar `FUN_003a83d8` pins every `HDR and Bloom` key to its field). Not modelled, stated in the module header: the event flash, the feedback mix, `fullscreenTintColour`, the radial bloom set, and the final display encode (still ADR-0026's `pow(1/2.2)` stand-in - the original's resolve ends on `ADD_SAT` and how the display consumes that is undecided). Measured at the reference grid framing: mean luminance 0.573 vs the reference's 0.586; clipped share 17.2 % vs 10.2 % - the overshoot tracks scene-magnitude fidelity (our lit surfaces run hotter than the original's), not the chain. **A wrongly-saturated glow mask is the trap to remember**: a downsample that writes `alpha = 1.0` hands the gate's alpha term the whole frame, and the picture it produces (a wall of white) looks exactly like a wrong gate formula. Next, in payoff order: the iridescent/facing material class (mechanism fully read: `etched_glass_tech` samples its gradient at `dot(N, V)`, a decoded dependent read; material rows name their own `.rcsmaterial`, so per-material classification at load is possible), and the second-texture emissive variants. **Settled 2026-08-19, no longer open**: the 56 unaddressed mesh nodes stay honest absence *and cost the picture nothing* - the visible sky traffic is the 33 world-space `animating_traffic` chunks the second pass already draws, and the nodes were never it (see rcsmodel.md, "Wipeout HD's road is not in the `.vex`"). What that investigation surfaced instead: **all 423 pad chunks on the disc were drawn by nobody** - a `Weapon Pad`/`Speedup Pad` node names its chunk at the mesh payload's own `+0x30`, `referenced()` counted every mentioned hash as "drawn through its node", and the node pass draws only the `Mesh` class. The chunks are world-space baked (measured: chunk centre beside node translation on all 9 Talons weapon pads and all 27 Anulpha speedup pads), so the fix was to narrow `referenced()` to what the node pass consumes and let the world-space pass have them.

## Open

- Whether `fogColour.w` is `Fog.Fog Density` unscaled is still unconfirmed. **Narrowed 2026-08-26, in two passes.** First: `Fog.Fog Density` lands on `+0x500` of the HDR/Bloom/Fog/Lighting settings singleton. Second, same day: `fogColour` is **engine parameter slot 7** of a *different*, already-documented mechanism (`docs/ghidra/functions/ps3-hdfury-eu/renderer.md`, "The engine's own parameter table, read from its initialiser") - its value-pointer field sits at a concrete address, `+0x40f8` of a runtime struct whose pointer lives at static storage `0x008b7f04`. What writes that pointer is still not found. `Material_SetInstanceParamPointer`, the obvious candidate, is *ruled out* (it explicitly skips every entry whose kind has the bit `0xc000` set, which is every engine parameter's kind by construction). **The "two functions load `0x008b7f04`" lead is itself refuted, 2026-09-01**: their `-0x54c0(r2)` was assumed to share `Shader_InitEngineParams`'s TOC without checking, and it doesn't - reading each function's own `.opd` entry directly gives TOC `0x008AD4D8` for both, against `Shader_InitEngineParams`'s `0x008BD3C4`, so their load resolves to a *different* static storage, `0x008A8018`, unrelated to fogColour. See renderer.md, "The 'two addresses' lead is refuted", for the full arithmetic and what that address turns out to hold instead (a pointer into an unidentified data table in the project's vtable address range). The real search - a `-0x54c0(r2)` load inside a function whose *own* `.opd` TOC is `0x008bd3c4` - has not been run.
- The sky rotation's sign is unverified - nothing on the disc checks it
- HD's exposure stage (adaptive exposure) is unread - a saturate-and-encode stand-in is used instead
- The iridescent/facing material (`etched_glass_tech`, `dot(N, V)` sampling) and the second-texture emissive variants are read but not implemented. **This is now also `talons-junctions-missing-floor-is-a-glass-floor.md`'s Next Steps item** ("wire up sampling of the normal map, specular map, and facing ramp roles") - the two threads converge on the same renderer work, which is a multi-sitting bind-group change (`crates/render/src/mesh.rs` sits at the 1,000-line cap and the mesh pipeline is already at wgpu's four-bind-group downlevel limit), not attempted here.
