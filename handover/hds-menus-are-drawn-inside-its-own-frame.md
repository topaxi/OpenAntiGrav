# HD's menus are drawn inside its own frame, and the `HD_*` palette turned out to be the FE style

2026-08-19, **the palette is the finding - it arrived by a disc-backed test failing.** *The frame*: HD authors the rules above and below every page (`line.gtf`, an 8x8 tile stretched to `1600x8`, at `y=110` and `y=975`), the `Title_Arrow_HD.gtf` mark at `(160, 73)` and the colour the page is cleared to, all on **one screen** - `FE Screen`, the parent every menu screen is nested inside. So `oag_title::FrontEnd::menu_frame` names *the screen* and `oag_game::menu::frame` reads the widgets off it at boot: **nothing in this repository holds one of those coordinates**, which is `CLAUDE.md`'s hand-transcribed-table rule applied to chrome. Its `<Text>` widgets are deliberately not drawn - they are the trial build's (`FE_TRIAL_MODE`, `FE_PURCHASE_NOW`) or belong to a `<NavigationController>` this build has no equivalent for, while the three images and the clear carry no such owner. **The palette**: reading `DATA06` says HD's front end is *white* (`HD_BG 0xffffffff`, `HD_Grey 0xff646464`, `HD_Blue 0xff8ac0ca` teal), and on that reading `FEGlobals->TextColor` - white - cannot be what its rows are drawn in, so the first cut of this change moved `MenuSkin::text` to grey. **Then the ground-truth test failed**: the archive the boot actually serves is `DATA00`, which declares the same six globals *black and red* (`HD_BG 0xff000000`, `HD_Grey 0xff969696`, `HD_Blue 0xffac0717`). Those are Fury and HD, `additional_definition.xml` has an `OPT_FE_STYLE` list whose entries are literally `HD` and `FURY`, and `DATA00` is the archive holding the Fury circuits and the `_fury` reel. **Confidence 88**, and what is unread is the code that chooses. Three consequences: `text` went back to `TextColor` (both archives agree on it and on `TitleColor`, which is why those two can be values); `selected` stays `None` **even though the disc states a highlight** - 25 `highlightColor` attributes, every one `HD_Blue` beside a `color` of `HD_Grey` - because its value is the style; and **this build shows the Fury palette** because `DATA00` serves the root, which is a choice of archive rather than a reading. The menu title is drawn in the *frame's own ink* rather than in `TitleColor`, which is read rather than inferred: HD's `Main Menu` authors its title `color="FEGlobals->HD_Grey"` and the frame's three widgets carry that same global, so taking it from the marks resolves it per archive. **Two traps worth carrying.** (1) `Screens` did not parse `<ScreenClear>` at all - the only fill it knew was the `<Image>`-with-a-colour spelling - so the field is new and nothing else reads it, which is what made adding it safe on the boot path. (2) A test that reads "the disc" through one archive is reading *an* archive: this one asserted `DATA06`'s values, passed nothing, and was wrong about which game's front end this build draws. `crates/game/tests/hd_menu_ground_truth.rs` now asserts the two archives *disagree*, so the day they stop is a failure. Reported at boot as `menu frame FE Screen: clears to #000000, 3 mark(s), ink #969696`. `crates/game/src/menu/frame.rs` (4 unit tests, 6 disc-backed), [hd-frontend.md](../docs/formats/hd-frontend.md#the-hd_-palette-is-the-fe-style-and-the-archives-disagree). **Still open**: whether the FE Style option rebinds the globals or swaps the served `skin.xml`; both PSP titles' `menu_frame` is `None`, and Pulse's is a *gap* rather than a measurement - its own `FE Screen` carries the light angled top bar that `menu::Skin::title_color` substitutes a colour for, and naming that screen would put the bar on a picture this build already draws, so it wants its own before-and-after.
