# Weapons: four of thirteen are built, and the fourth is the first one that is *recovered* rather than half-invented

2026-08-17, [missile.md](../docs/ghidra/functions/psp-pulse-usa/missile.md) and [pickups.md](../docs/gameplay/pickups.md) - the pages to read, not this row. A `Weapon Pad` hands out a pickup, `SQUARE` fires it, `CIRCLE` absorbs it, a time trial gets its free turbo a lap, and **Turbo, Shield, Rocket and Missile** do something. **The Missile is the interesting one.** Its blocker was recorded here for months as "needs a lock and a target, which needs the AI", and the AI was never what it needed: the lock is authored (`lock_min_dist`/`lock_max_dist`) and *everything else* is readable. Recovered on 2026-08-17 and ported whole: the `<Stats>` offsets (`WeaponStats_ParseMissile` `0x0880c31c`, 90); that a press fires **one**, not three (`Weapon_FireMissile` `0x088685cc`, bit `0x40`, 90); the lock (`Ship_AcquireLock` `0x08844784`, 88) - a **longitudinal** window rather than a range, `0.9` cone, `1.4` gap-over-range screen, nearest-by-longitudinal-distance wins; the guidance (`Missile_Update` `0x0885a918`, 95) - a chord-clamped move-towards at `dt * 4.0`, **no trigonometry at all**, applied a tick late, and the direction deliberately not renormalised so a turning missile flies *slower*; the speed ramp (`Missile_SpeedNow` `0x0885a038`, 90) - a hardcoded one second from the launcher's own speed to the class speed; and the flight - the Rocket's model with a **12.0** probe instead of 6.0, and **five wall bounces** where a rocket dies on the first. **Two things fell out on the way.** (1) **What `launchSpeed` is for**, which `projectile.rs` had recorded as unfound: a muzzle velocity added to the *launcher's* speed, with the class speed being where the ramp ends. Deliberately **not** propagated to the Rocket, which has its own `Rocket_Init` and was not re-read. (2) **The pickup-grant call site** - `WeaponPickup_Grant` (`0x08861d20`), recorded across three passes as not existing: a `rand() % total` cumulative walk, with a separate AI path lerping two weight columns by race position and refusing to repeat the last weapon. **The table *is* `<Pickupodds>` at 92**: `WeaponStats_ParsePickupOdds` (`0x0880e93c`) writes its weights to exactly the offsets the grant walks, and `WeaponStats_Parse`'s tail sums them into the total it divides by. The layout is `weapon_base + attribute*0x10 + class*4`, attributes `ai`/`human`/`front`/`back`, totals at `0x4b8`..`0x4e8`. **Three "ours" rows on `pickups.md` are retired by it** - that a crossing grants anything, the weighted draw, and the one-slot inventory. **Ported the same day**: `pickup::draw` spends the `ai` column flat, blends the player's `human` column with `front`/`back` by place, and refuses to repeat the last grant (`Held::last`, hashed). Second isolated hash move of the day. **The blend is the interesting part and reads the opposite way round from the obvious guess**: the branch that consults `front`/`back` is the *human's*, `human + back*t + front*(1-t)` with `t = (place-1)/ship_count`, so the leader draws more Shields and the tail more Turbos. The pickup draw rubber-bands the **player**, not the field. **And one live doc claim was wrong**: `weapon-fire.md` read `craft+0x1bc` as a target; it is the **held-weapon slot** (`-1` = empty, and the arm functions write weapon ids `0` and `1` to it). Corrected on both pages. Note `+0x1c0` at that base is *not* `engine.md`'s eleven-bit flag word - different struct, and conflating them will waste a session. **What is still out is nine weapons, and each is blocked on something specific**: Quake needs track deformation, LeachBeam a beam, most of the rest the slowdown mechanic behind `<Global slowdown_limit>` - **whose consumer is now known** (`weapon_record+0x130`, accumulated by a missile impact) even though nothing spends it. Autopilot is weapon id 6, bit `0x1000`, and is driver work. `oag_gameplay::pickup::IMPLEMENTED` is still the whole list; growing it is still the whole change, and `every_implemented_weapon_has_a_fire_arm_on_both_paths` is now what stops that being a silent no-op - the old `_ => return` comment claimed to be a compile error and was not. **Verified**: `just` green at **1,949**, and four disc-backed tests in the new `crates/game/tests/missile_ground_truth.rs` (a missile closes on its target against a straight-shot counterfactual, never locks its firer, glances off the circuit's own barriers, and a pad can hand one out). The world hash moved once, isolated the documented way - previous constants reproduce bit for bit with only the three new `Projectile` fields' writes removed.

## Open

- Nine of thirteen weapons are still unbuilt: Quake needs track deformation, LeachBeam needs a beam, and most of the rest need the slowdown mechanic behind `<Global slowdown_limit>`.
- `slowdown_limit`'s consumer (`weapon_record+0x130`, accumulated by a missile impact) is known but nothing spends it yet.
- Autopilot (weapon id 6, bit `0x1000`) still needs driver work.
- Whether `launchSpeed` applies to the Rocket is unconfirmed - it has its own `Rocket_Init`, which was not re-read.

## Next Steps

- Build the remaining nine weapons against their specific blockers (track deformation, a beam, the slowdown consumer, driver work for Autopilot).
- Grow `oag_gameplay::pickup::IMPLEMENTED`, now guarded by `every_implemented_weapon_has_a_fire_arm_on_both_paths` so an unhandled weapon fails loudly instead of silently no-opping.
