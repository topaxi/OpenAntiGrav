# An own track format, with BallisticNG and the originals as importers into it

2026-10-11, the maintainer's idea after the Unity content support phases: for custom
tracks, this project's **own** format should be the canonical one, and a foreign track
format (BallisticNG's `.trk`, read by `oag-ballisticng` on top of `oag-unity`) becomes one
importer into it rather than the format custom tracks are authored in. **Queued to start
right after Unity content support phase 2 lands** (`unity-content-support-phase-2.md`).
Nothing below is built; it is a design to check against the code before building.

## Why

- **No lossy mapping.** The format holds what the race consumes: the spline frame, floor
  and wall collision, pads, the start grid, the lap line, the AI line, visual meshes and
  their materials. The chosen constants a BallisticNG track needs (scale about 8.7,
  handedness, tile codes 1-4) do not exist for a track authored in it.
- **Ours to version, keep deterministic and extend** per title: Zone, Pulse's pad kinds,
  HD's emissive pad masks, sound emitters, scenery animation.
- **Open tooling.** Geometry as glTF 2.0 (Blender and Unity both export it; `extras` carries
  per-mesh tags such as floor/wall/scenery), race data as a small TOML beside it. No
  proprietary engine is needed to author one.

BallisticNG keeps its value as a library: an existing community and a large Workshop. Our
format starts with no tracks and no authoring tool; a Blender add-on is the real cost, not
the reader.

## Landed 2026-10-11

`oag-trackfile` (ADR-0063, `docs/formats/track-file.md`): the model is `oag_trackfile::Track` (the mapping's output
moved into it), glb for every simulation float plus a TOML manifest, `oag-game --track-file`, the local disc exporter
(`cargo run -p oag-raceplay --example export_track`), a committed fixture (`crates/trackfile/tests/fixtures/oval.*`,
regenerated by `cargo run -p oag-trackfile --example make_fixture`). Ground truth in
`crates/raceplay/tests/trackfile_ground_truth.rs`: 80 of 80 Pulse circuits, 27 of 27 Workshop Originals and four
other titles' default circuits round-trip equal; lone-craft per-tick hashes equal on two Pulse circuits and Lion's Den.

Also landed 2026-10-12: the track picker. RACE REMIX's TRACK TITLE offers CUSTOM, listing every `.wtrk` and
`*.track.toml` the `--ballisticng-track` directories hold (`docs/architecture/menus.md`, "RACE REMIX: CUSTOM tracks").
Broken files are listed dimmed with their reason; the menu launch and `--ballisticng-track` hash the same on Lion's Den.

Landed 2026-10-12: the Blender add-on (`scripts/blender/oag_track`, `docs/tools/blender.md`): tags, spline sampling,
floor and wall builder, start and pads, importer and exporter; a loop built from scratch and the oval re-exported
are committed fixtures (`just blender-fixtures`), read by `crates/trackfile/tests/blender.rs` and raced by two new
`trackfile_ground_truth` cases.

## Open

- **Closed 2026-10-27 (`own-tracks-row`):** RACE REMIX's CUSTOM title lists own-format tracks in every build
  (one title; the off-by-default `ballisticng` feature only adds the Workshop rows). Test:
  `custom_tracks::tests::an_own_format_track_is_listed_raceable_and_starts_a_race_whatever_the_features`.

- Add-on follow-ups (`docs/tools/blender.md`, "Open"): banking and per-point width on the curve; multi-path and
  junction authoring beyond the scene property the importer writes.
- Visual meshes, materials, sky, fog, lights, emitters and PVS as optional records (a minor version each); today a track
  file draws as flat-shaded collision and the report names the absent list.
- Hosts other than Pulse (HD, 2048, Omega physics) are not exercised for a raced track file.
