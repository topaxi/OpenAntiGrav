# A chunk header is 0x20 bytes and then a *surface* record, and the byte after the layout says which space it is in

2026-08-25, **landed**. Both findings, all counts and the guards are on [rcsmodel.md](../../docs/formats/rcsmodel.md); executable side on [visibility.md](../../docs/ghidra/functions/ps3-hdfury-eu/visibility.md); pinned by `rcsmodel_surface_ground_truth.rs`. **The surface table**: material, bias, scale, count, decl and descriptors are fields of a 0x40-byte record at chunk `+0x20` that **recurs for every surface past the first**, listed at chunk `+0x18`, whose first entry points at `chunk + 0x20` on all 41,861 chunks. **9,891 (24%) carry more**, each naming another material, holding **25,972 submeshes and 7,111,578 triangles (+40.6%)** that nothing drew. Talon's went 471,024 -> **809,247** triangles; 44% of Anulpha's start-line frame changed, and the barrier hex panelling and the reference's silver structure appeared for the first time. **Why the obvious fix would have been wrong**: a surface is not a submesh (503 of 9,891 have equal counts) - geometry, not a relabelling. **How it stayed cheap**: a surface *is* a `Mesh`, so `extra_surfaces` + `surfaces()` left 213 call sites untouched. **Two guards not to remove**: a later surface that will not read is skipped rather than failing its chunk; and the node pass applies `submesh_fits` **only to the chunk's own surface**. **Chunk byte `+0x07`** says which **space** a chunk is in - `1` world-baked (33,088), `2` node-local (8,773) - replacing `is_world_baked`, a heuristic invented here with a 1.0-unit tolerance. Judged by the bias, independent of both, **the byte is right 94.4% and the heuristic 43.2%**, and the bias sides with the byte on *both* directions of disagreement. Talon's 60 -> 70 node-drawn with zero misfiled, frame byte-identical; Anulpha 101 -> 113. The executable confirms it (`Scene_RefreshNodeMatrices` copies a 4x4 from a linked node each frame, kind 2 only) and gives a **clean negative**: no arm of either switch touches the declaration, index buffer, stride or surface count. **`+0x05` is read by nothing**, closing the old "nn counts chunks" note at 85. **Not modelled**: the submit folds the kind into a sort key and excludes kind-2 chunks from two of five draw buckets. Opcodes `0x12`/`0x16`/`0x03` in the load arms are unidentified and deliberately unguessed.
