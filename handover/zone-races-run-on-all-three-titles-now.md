# Zone races run on all three titles now, and the craft axis closed the way the circuit one did

2026-08-19, same branch. `--race --mode zone` loads and drives on Pulse, Pure and HD/Fury, each with its own look - Pulse cyan/teal, Pure white/lavender, HD the white ribbon. **The craft axis became a type the moment HD was measured**, and the sequence is the point: it was refused a day earlier at two-of-three (Pulse and Pure measured, HD unread), then HD's answer turned out to be `/data/ships/zone/ship.vex` - already on the roster as one of `oag_hd::names::MODE_SHIPS`, listed before anything wanted it - and three-with-no-hole is what ADR-0022 licenses. `oag_title::ZoneCraft` is the result and **it splits the corpus exactly the way `ZoneCircuit` does**: Pulse hangs Zone off the entities a race already has (the player's team gains `Zone.vex`, the race circuit gains `zone_track.vex`), Pure and HD give Zone entities of its own (its own ship directory, its own circuits). One decision per title, showing up twice. Pulse's is the only branch with a recovered *selector* (`Ship_LoadModel` `case 6`, 84); the other two are name resolution at 94 and neither executable has been read. **The finding that does not fit the enum, and is the one worth reading**: all three titles ship a Zone-mode craft *directory* whose `handlingstats.xml` opens `<Stats team="ZoneMode">` and authors **no `<Class>` block at all**, where a team file authors four or five - `Data\Ships\Zone_01` on *both* PSP titles (Pulse has one too, which was the surprise) and `/data/ships/zone` on HD. So the shipped answer to "what handling does a Zone craft have" is **one block shared by every team**, which fits a mode that replaces the engine with the auto-speed law, disables the brakes and flies a four-corner hover variant - exactly what a `<Class>` would otherwise carry. **This engine does not read it**: `oag_gameplay::handling_for` `.expect()`s a class and would panic, so a Zone race still flies the player's own team's numbers and `race::load` prints that divergence on every Zone load. Closing it is a physics question (which blocks the mode supplies, and where turning comes from when no class authors one), not a naming one. **Trap**: `Data\Ships\Zone` on Pulse is `<Stats team="Zone">` **with** classes and is the unlockable Zone *livery*, a raceable team - `Zone_01` is the mode, and the two differ by a suffix. **What each title still lacks** is unchanged by this and pre-existing: Pure ships no boost plume under any recovered name (reported, race runs without one), HD draws its hulls untextured off `.rcsmodel` with computed normals, and both PSP titles' Zone HUD draws its labels with no values behind them (the `IG_HUD_*` substitution gap). `crates/game/tests/zone_ground_truth.rs` is eight tests across four discs.
