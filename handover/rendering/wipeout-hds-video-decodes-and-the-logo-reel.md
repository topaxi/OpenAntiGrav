# Wipeout HD's video decodes, and the logo reel is the Studio Liverpool ident

2026-08-17, the sibling of the soundtrack and built the same way: read the container, hand the codec to what already decodes codecs. **HD's video is RAD's Bink**, 37 files and 101.2 MiB - two 1080p studio logo reels, 16 circuit previews at 352x256, 19 front-end mode icons - and `oag_video::bik` reads the header on all 37 with three arithmetic invariants each. **The finding worth carrying is that a `.bik` is little-endian**, alone among HD's payloads: everything else on the disc is Pulse's format byte-swapped, and this one is not, because the container is the PC authoring tool's rather than the console's. Anyone porting a reader to HD by adding a `ByteOrder` argument will reach for one here and get `0x80070000` pixels wide. **[ADR-0024](../../docs/architecture/adr/0024-in-process-codecs-and-ffmpeg-as-a-last-resort.md)'s category check was made rather than inherited**, which is the thing that ADR asks for and the reason it exists: category 1 has exactly one candidate, `infinitier_bik_decoder` 0.0.14, and it is **GPL-3.0-or-later**, which the licence bar excludes - `nihav` has Bink decoders and is not on crates.io at all; category 2 is closed by the rule, Bink being RAD's format and not Wipeout's. So category 3, and the transcode is nine lines around the cache that was already there. The **container header** is still read here, as `.PMF`'s and `.IPF`'s are, and that is what buys the degradation a program stream cannot offer: with no `ffmpeg` at all a `.bik` still reports a correct frame count, size and rate. **Cost, measured before wiring anything**: the 531-frame 1080p reel is ~98 s of `libaom` and 21 MiB of cache, once - same order as the PSP intro at a twenty-sixth of the pixels. **What decoding it settled**: `oag_hd::frontend::names::STUDIO_LOGO_MOVIE`'s doc comment recorded that the name was the whole of the evidence for what the file held; it is the Studio Liverpool ident, 8.86 s resolving out of black to a `STUDIO Liverpool` card. What it did *not* settle is which of the two reels a PS3 plays - that is still the which-`skin.xml` question. `crates/game/tests/hd_movie_ground_truth.rs` (3 tests, all disc-backed), [bik.md](../../docs/formats/bik.md). **What is no longer open**: the app draws a `.bik` - `just play hd` plays the reel through HD's own chain, and `--movie <path>` reaches any of the 37 - and, since 2026-09-21, **the audio plays too**: a third `MovieAudioKind`, `Container`, holds a path to the cached `.bik` file `ffmpeg` already reads for the picture, and a second `ffmpeg` call pulls the audio stream out of the same container with `-map`. The boot prints `audio: 2 channel(s) at 48000 Hz, binkaudio_dct, track 0 of 4 (chosen, not measured), decoded to 8.88s` where it used to print `audio: Studio Logo plays silently`. See [bik.md](../../docs/formats/bik.md#the-audio-is-inside-the-video-file).
