## Open

Ranked by what a player notices.

1. **The frame-wide darkness is mostly closed, 2026-10-05 (`hd-darkness`): HD's lightmap was sRGB-decoded in front of the authored curve, which the microcode does not do** (confidence 85; [renderer.md](../../docs/ghidra/functions/ps3-hdfury-eu/renderer.md), "HD's lightmap is read raw"). The darkness sat entirely on the lightmapped materials (Amphiseum power 3.5 compounded to 7.7), not on a missing light. Luma ours / reference, before -> after: Talon's 00 0.404 -> 0.533 / 0.618, Amphiseum 00 0.164 -> 0.237 / 0.245, Sol 2 00 0.493 -> 0.580 / 0.684. **What remains**: Talon's -0.09 to -0.14, Sol 2 -0.09 to -0.26 (the light-sum domain: the authored branch encodes `L^0.4545` where the original's raw multiply carries `L`, and the exposure/encode end, question 1 in renderer.md's "Per-texture sRGB/`GAMMA` decode"), and Amphiseum 01/02 now overshoot by +0.10/+0.05 (01's billboards are white where the reference is saturated red-orange, an emissive combine).
2. ~~**Sky luma changes sign between circuits**~~ **closed 2026-10-07 (`hd-sky-luma`)**: it was one circuit, and the box. Measured on the cube's own pixels (a render with the cube skipped marks them), Talon's and Amphiseum already matched and only Sol 2 was dark, by 1.8x; Amphiseum's gap was its ceiling in the `sky` box. Cause: `Lighting.Sky colour` is the cube's vertex colour (128 on Talon's, 255 on Sol 2 and the other seven Fury/DLC circuits), first applied as `byte / 128` (a fit through two circuits); **replaced 2026-10-08 (`hd-sky-law`) by `byte / 255`, measured off live draws: the factor of two was the race-start exposure, not the sky**. Sol 2 00 0.479 -> 0.875 against 0.876, 02 0.651 -> 0.985 against 0.983, Talon's and Amphiseum unchanged. The brief's "tone keys identical" held only through the front end's carried triple (Sol 2's file omits two of the three). Evidence: renderer.md, "HD's sky is tinted by `Lighting.Sky colour`". Sol 2 whole-frame luma before -> after (reference): 00 0.579 -> 0.589 (0.684), 01 0.566 -> 0.630 (0.698), 02 0.653 -> 0.793 (0.911), so item 1's Sol 2 gap shrinks by a quarter to a half on 01/02. **Still open**: the divisor 128 is a two-circuit fit confounded with archive (DATA00 vs DATA02); the matched Vineta K pose 00 (byte 134) shows no doubling (0.422 vs 0.413) but its open sky is near white in both, so it is weak - a matched mid-tone-sky frame on another 255 circuit settles it; where the factor of two comes from in the executable (the prim-list programs read are unscaled), the other seven DLC circuits have no matched frame, and Sol 2 01 is 0.731 against 0.807.
