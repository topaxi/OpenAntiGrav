Method: `scripts/hd-frame-compare.py` over the three matched sets (`talons-matched` 00/01/03, `amphiseum-matched` 00/01/02, `sol2-matched` 00/01/02) on this tree before and after, frames read as side-by-side crops at player size. ## Open

Ranked by what a player notices.

1. **The frame is darker than the original on Talon's Junction (all three poses, -0.21 to -0.22 luma) and Sol 2 (-0.19 to -0.30), and Amphiseum pose 00 (-0.08).** Two earlier lanes cleared the post chain, the exposure stage and the `.envsettings` plumbing (renderer.md, "The darkness gap does not reduce to an offset, a scale, or a single curve"). New this pass, and it narrows the cause to light, not art: on Amphiseum pose 00 the road is `track_surface.rcsmaterial` (slot 547, found by `OAG_TINT_MATERIALS` hue match), its albedo is light grey under `OAG_ALBEDO_ONLY`, yet the lit frame is near black where the reference floor is lit cyan. Same direction on Sol 2 pose 02, where the reference is a near white-out (mean 0.911, 68.6 % clipped, against ours 0.611 and 5.8 %). Amphiseum pose 01 is the exception that matches (0.469 vs 0.457), so this is not a global scale. The light that is missing on that floor is the likeliest candidate: the `Enable_spu_vertex_light` thread (`hds-frame-was-too-bright-and-too-bloomy.md`) has the SPU `EdgeGeom` light accumulation read and wired for ships but the track's own lights unaccounted for. Next measurement: per-material luma deltas with `scripts/hd-material-probe.py` on Amphiseum pose 00, which says whether the floor alone carries the gap.
2. **Sky luma changes sign between circuits** (Sol 2 pose 00: ours 0.497, reference 0.861; Talon's Junction pose 03: 0.370 vs 0.656; Talon's Junction pose 00 and Amphiseum are close). Tone keys are identical on all three circuits (`Tone adaption boost` 20, `clamp` 3, `maximum` 4), so the difference is not authored exposure; `Lighting.Sky colour` (128 Talon's, 140 Amphiseum) is the per-circuit input to check against the sky cube's draw.
3. **Bloom/glare is under-reproduced.** The reference has a soft glare around every bright source (see the white upper-right of `sol2-matched/02`); ours reads flat. `[graphics] bloom` is inert for HD, so this is the resolve chain's own gain, listed in `hds-frame-was-too-bright-and-too-bloomy.md`.
4. **Not compared this pass, still open in their own threads**: the hull wash on boost (`hds-engine-light-washes-the-hull-on-boost.md`, the original does not wash a `Distance = 0.0` hull, confidence 75), pad emissive mask, culled weapon back faces, weapon models, the glass `c` term and paraboloid tint (`hd-needs-a-per-material-shader-path-and.md`).
5. **Harness note**: `scripts/hd-frame-compare.py` writes `<pair-dir>-compare/` beside its input. Run from a worktree whose `data/` is a symlink, that overwrote the shared `data/reference/hd-capture/{talons,sol2,amphiseum}-matched-compare` images (the 09-13 `00`/`01` frames). Point `--pair-dir` at a copy under scratch, as this pass did (`data/scratch/hd-accuracy/cmp.sh`).
