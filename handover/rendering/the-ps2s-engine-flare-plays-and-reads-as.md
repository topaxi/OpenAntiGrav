# The PS2's engine flare plays and reads as nothing on screen

2026-08-12. `WO_SHIP_ENGINEFLARE` is wired, attaches per craft, and **is** drawing (the nozzle glow changes with `psys::ColourScale`) - a maintainer just sees only the trail ribbon. The asset side is closed: both engine-flare textures are on the PS2 archive after all, under the declared name with the extension rewritten to `.pct` (`Engine_noise` 64x64 8bpp, `grabbedEngineFlare128x64x8` 128x64 8bpp) - a name-lookup gap, not an asset gap. See [texture-names.md](../../docs/ghidra/functions/ps2-pulse-eu/texture-names.md). **The related boost-plume half is closed**: [The PS2 boost plume draws, and the PS2 file is not the PSP file wearing the same name](the-ps2-boost-plume-draws-and-the-ps2.md), dated 2026-08-23. Both are render-side, neither touches the simulation.

**2026-09-03: both remaining questions answered with real frame captures, and the thread is closing.**

**On our own build**: `just play ps2 --race --mode time_trial --hold cross --ticks 340 --screenshot t.png` (headless, no window - `--screenshot` never opens one) against the same command with `--hold cross` left out. `COUNTDOWN_TICKS = 272` (`crates/race/src/state.rs`) gates thrust at the start line, so 340 ticks is enough to actually be moving in the held-thrust capture (213 km/h) and stationary in the other. The nozzle glow is unmistakably different between the two, not merely "the trail ribbon" as the original note read it: a wide white/violet halo at rest, a small pink/magenta core once under thrust and moving. Confirmed directly, trivially reproducible with the commands above.

**On the real PS2 build**: the PCSX2 harness built for the boost-plume thread (`docs/reverse-engineering/pcsx2-debugger.md`, dated 2026-08-23) was not yet usable this session - `~/.config/PCSX2/bios` was empty, so `just pcsx2-boot` failed at `ReportErrorAsync: Startup Error: PCSX2 requires a PlayStation 2 BIOS`, a precondition the doc page assumed rather than stated (now fixed there). With a PAL BIOS placed in that folder, the harness booted straight to `SCES-54748` in 3 s and the documented front-end walk (autosave -> 50 Hz prompt -> title -> name/tag entry -> format card -> save -> Race Campaign -> Grid 1 -> Single Race, Moa Therma White, Venom, 3 laps -> Assegai) reproduced exactly as the doc's table describes it, at every one of the fourteen steps, on the first attempt. A savestate was taken on the grid (`just pcsx2-state save 2`), so a capture from here on is one command (`just pcsx2-frame out.png <frames> cross --from-state 2`), not the four-minute walk. **The real disc draws the same shape of effect**: a bright core at the nozzle with a magenta/pink streak below it, present stationary on the grid and growing over the first few seconds of continuous thrust. Confidence 70 that the effect is present and reactive at all - one capture, one savestate.

**What "strength at a given velocity" actually asked, and why the screenshot sweep that followed doesn't answer it.** A same-day follow-up swept both builds across several speeds (our own build: held thrust to 67/213/376 km/h past `COUNTDOWN_TICKS`; the real disc: `pcsx2-frame` at 20/45/90/200 frames from the grid state, 30 to ~280 km/h) looking for a matching size-vs-speed curve, and found the opposite of what was expected: our build's glow was *largest* at the lowest speed (67 km/h, moments after the gate lifts) and visibly smaller at 213 and 376 km/h, while the PCSX2 sweep looked like steady growth. **Neither reading survives scrutiny, for three independent reasons, and the formula settles it instead of the pixels:**

1. `Exhaust::advance` (`crates/render/src/exhaust.rs:443-497`) computes `half_size` from **`intensity`** (a plain 0-to-1 ramp over `engine_on`, `+dt*0.25`/`-dt*0.5`, saturating after four seconds of continuous thrust) and **`boost_timer`** (armed only by a pickup or pad, `exhaust::BOOST_SECONDS` = 0.8 s, worth up to 8x the intensity term while it lasts) - `speed_kmh`/`speed_ramp()` is computed every tick but **is not read by `half_size` at all**. This is already correctly documented, not a gap found here: `boost_accumulator()`'s own doc comment says "nothing in the original's *visual* path was found reading it - the size uses the boost timer instead."
2. That is also what the original does. `docs/ghidra/functions/psp-pulse-usa/exhaust.md`'s `Exhaust_UpdateEngineSound` section (confidence 80-82) recovers `target = base + speed_kmh * 5.0` as the **engine note's pitch**, explicitly *not* a size - "worth saying so loudly because it reads exactly like a flame length" and isn't one. The shared visual intensity `i` that function also maintains ramps on `engine_on` alone, the same shape `exhaust.rs` reproduces. So **"grows with speed" is the wrong frame for either build**: the flare's strength tracks continuous-thrust *duration* (and a pickup/pad boost), not velocity, by design, on both sides. Velocity and duration are merely correlated in ordinary play, which is what made the screenshot sweep look speed-shaped at first.
3. The screenshot sweep also carries three confounds a formula reading doesn't: the capture points sit in visibly different scene lighting (an underpass vs. open sky), which changes how an additive glow reads against its background independent of its true size; the PS2's own `Aspect Ratio` option genuinely widens the 3D camera by `4/3` rather than post-stretching the frame (`docs/ps2/aspect-ratio.md`, confidence 95-98), flagged directly by the maintainer mid-session, and which of the two the memory-card profile used for the PCSX2 captures was never checked; and, also flagged by the maintainer and confirmed by source, **this project's own race camera never reproduces that option on any title** - its FOV is fitted to a single hardcoded PSP-authored constant regardless of which disc is loaded, a distinct and still-open question from the one the front-end-layout thread already closed. See [The race camera's FOV is fitted to the PSP's own aspect on every title, and the PS2's own in-game widening option has no counterpart](the-race-cameras-fov-is-fitted-to-the-psps.md). Three more reasons a same-pixels-different-size reading proves nothing on its own.

So: the mechanism is confirmed to match by design and by source (confidence 80-90, both sides), and the pixel sweep that looked like a mismatch was measuring the wrong variable through two uncontrolled confounds - it is not evidence of anything and should not be read as either confirming or refuting fidelity.

Neither capture is committed - both are derived evidence and gitignored, per the standing rule; the commands above reproduce them from a clean checkout (plus, for the PCSX2 half, the harness's own BIOS precondition).

## Open

- No pixel-level comparison of the flare's exact colour or alpha has been made between the two builds - only that the governing formula (intensity + boost timer, no direct speed term) is confirmed to match by source on both sides.
- Which `Aspect Ratio` setting (`4:3` vs `16:9`) the PCSX2 harness's memory-card profile is using has never been checked or pinned - see `docs/ps2/aspect-ratio.md`. Any future pixel/size comparison against a PS2 capture needs this controlled first, and matched against our own render at the same camera aspect.

## Next Steps

- If a pixel-level comparison is ever wanted, pin the PS2 profile's `Aspect Ratio` to a known value first (`docs/ps2/aspect-ratio.md`), match our own render's viewport aspect to it, and hold both captures to the same scene lighting (same track position) - a same-speed comparison without controlling those three is not a comparison.
- Do not tune the flare to taste - treat a wrong-looking result as a missing mechanism, not a coefficient, once a real comparison like that exists.
