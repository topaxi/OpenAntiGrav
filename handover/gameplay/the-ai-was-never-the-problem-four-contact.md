# The AI was never the problem: four contact-path bugs, and what is left after them

**2026-08-12, and the headline is that none of a five-section investigation in [ai.md](../../docs/gameplay/ai.md) was an AI fault.** The twelve-circuit solo benchmark (`a_lone_craft_gets_round_the_circuits_it_is_known_to_get_round`) went from **two circuits managing a clean lap to twelve**, eleven of them without a single recovery, and total recoveries across the disc went **22 to 2** (ai.md's own "Ungated" table). `Tuning::lateral_accel` **stayed at 180**: 220/260 gave faster clean laps and one more clean circuit, but respawns barely moved across the range (29 at grip 120, 35 at 180, 33 at 260) - a number that does not respond to grip is not answering a grip question, so it waits on the corner-departure investigation ai.md still has open. What was actually wrong, in the order it was found: (1) `Spline::from_track` concatenates **every** path in file order, so on `05`/`14`/`07` the AI line spliced in the far branch of a split - a kilometre away, facing backwards - and drivers stalled at it; `Course::path_order` and `Race::ai_order` walk the lap ring instead. (2) `Antigrav::rebound_jump_time` was parsed and **never read**: the original arms the landing response *in the air*, once the flight outlasts that parameter, so a short hop lands on ordinary `rebound`; this crate reset on every touchdown edge and so sat under `landing_rebound` almost permanently. (3) `Ship_CastHoverProbes` **branches on speed** (`craft+0x2ec <= 50.0`): above it the original casts one ray and *manufactures* the rear probe's whole hit record from the front one, copying the hit flag, so a front probe in contact guarantees a rear probe in contact and `grounded` cannot read `0.5`. Two independent casts at every speed shed half the suspension over every lip. (4) **The contact test only looks down.** Once a surface is above the hull no ray of any length finds it. `hover::sweep` is the answer and it is **ours, not recovered** - the only such mechanism in `oag-physics` - a swept test of each probe's motion segment across the tick. **Four traps, each of which cost real time.** (a) *A player driving the same section by hand fell through it too.* That single fact was available from the first report and would have ruled out the AI immediately; it was established five sections in. **Try it before a measurement campaign, not after.** (b) *The first `hover::sweep` was gated on `state.grounded == 0.0`, which is exactly wrong*: `grounded` is set **before** the integrator moves the craft, so on the one tick the hull crosses a surface it still reports the contact it had on the way in. The gate skipped the only case it existed for, and the benchmark moved by one recovery on one circuit - what a fix that never fires looks like. (c) *Two conclusions reached by reconstructing an event backwards were both wrong* - see the AI row's own trap list; the honest instrument watched the **first tick of lost contact**. (d) *`13_Track`'s 226 novice recoveries were filed as "the authored jump needs a minimum speed"* - a mechanism that fitted the symptom perfectly and was not the cause: it was falling through like everything else. **Three things still open.** *An opponent ignores speed pads entirely* - pad seeking was built and removed earlier because it cost lap time and broke the difficulty ordering, and it is wanted at the higher levels; it needs a fourth `Context` channel, as pad greed and dodging fire both do. *A craft that has simply stopped is recovered as of 2026-08-17* (`Race::stalled`, a second dwell on speed rather than distance; the measurement and the two constants are on [ai.md](../../docs/gameplay/ai.md)). **What that did not fix is the reason it was needed**: every place it fires is inside or just before a documented run of `05_Track` line samples with nothing under them, so a craft is put back and comes off again in the same place. **The racing line running above the collision surface is the live thread**, not the recovery. *The difficulty settings key and pre-race menu option* are believed done and were never verified end to end. **Resolved 2026-08-25**: `craft+0x208` is what `Collision_RaycastWorld` writes at its own hit record's `+0x28` - not a copy of the collider's surface type, but a fixed constant each of its two narrowphase raycasts writes about itself (`Collision_RaycastMesh` writes `1`, the newly-named `Collision_RaycastBox` writes `3`), and `crates/physics/src/hover.rs`'s escape gate already reproduces the intended behaviour via its own `Surface` enum, landed independently before this reading. The classifier that picks between the two raycasts (`collider+0x78`) turned out to be the same virtual read `Collision_DispatchPair` uses for its own pairing dispatch, confirmed byte-for-byte and cross-read against `Collision_BoxAgainstMesh`'s field accesses - `1` is a mesh-shaped collider, `3` a box-shaped one, and fixing that also corrected a pre-existing swapped `box`/`mesh` label on `collision.md`'s `Collision_DispatchPair` paragraph. See [engine.md](../../docs/ghidra/functions/psp-pulse-usa/engine.md#the-probes-depenetrate-as-well-as-push) and [collision.md](../../docs/ghidra/functions/psp-pulse-usa/collision.md#raycasts).

## Open

- Opponents ignore speed pads entirely - pad greed needs a fourth `Context` channel, the same one dodging fire needs
- The racing line runs above the collision surface on `05_Track`, which is why a stalled craft is put back and comes off again in the same place - the stall-recovery fix (`Race::stalled`) does not address this
- The difficulty settings key and pre-race menu option are believed done but were never verified end to end

## Next Steps

- Add a fourth `Context` channel so pad greed and dodging fire can be built
- Investigate why the racing line sits above the collision surface on `05_Track` - the live thread behind the stall recovery
