# A PS3 title now boots, drives and screenshots from a script - and its savestates are not worth adopting

2026-08-19/20, branch `worktree-rpcs3-harness`. **The whole harness and every trap it cost is on [rpcs3-debugger.md](../docs/reverse-engineering/rpcs3-debugger.md); the physics findings are on [physics.md](../docs/ghidra/functions/ps3-hdfury-eu/physics.md) and [race-manager.md](../docs/ghidra/functions/ps3-hdfury-eu/race-manager.md).** This row keeps only what those do not decide for you. **State**: `just rpcs3-race` boots HD on Xvfb, walks six taps of cross into a race and drives it, scripted, with no window; `just rpcs3-record` captures it as video with the HUD in frame, which is the closest thing to an M3 per-tick trace this platform has. `--headless` is a boot-and-read-memory mode only - the front end parks for good in `cellGameDataCheck`. Savestates work and were **rejected**: ~60 s to load, restoring to the Campaign screen, further from a race than the 45 s cold boot. Also recorded nowhere else - **a savestate is no use as an offline memory image**: it holds none of the EBOOT's read-only pages, so there is no ELF header to anchor file offsets to guest addresses with. **The three facts that shape anything built on this**: the GDB stub answers *nothing* while the target runs, so a polling client hangs rather than timing out; 41 ms a packet **paused** caps you at ~24 operations a second, so there is no per-tick capture on this transport; and a `Z0` breakpoint fires only under `PPU Decoder: Interpreter (static)`, parks its thread **without sending a stop reply**, and therefore reads as "this address never executes" - **a negative from `wait_for_stop()` is not a negative**, ask "is any thread's PC here" via `Debugger.wait_at()`. Two claims died to that trap; both are recorded on the docs page rather than repeated here. **What is known about the game**: the eight craft bodies are found and the player's throttle is exact (`craft+0x30c`, `100.00`/`0.00`, confidence 90), reproduced across three boots. **Speed is in neither object** on three scans, which is the standing negative - the HUD's km/h is probably computed at draw time from a velocity the renderer reaches elsewhere. **Next, in order**: the other three craft vtable slots (`+0x38`, `+0x40`, `+0x44`); the velocity `Collision_MarchSegment`'s caller must hold; then a committed input script and a lap, wall-clock paced. **No steering field is identified** - a 12-sample candidate at `entry+0x284` was refuted at 24 samples, and six samples correlate nothing (`r=-0.987` fell to `-0.040`): the methodological lesson worth carrying.
